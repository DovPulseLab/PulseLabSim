Aortic input impedance modulus

After Nichols & Edwards 2001, Fig. 1. Zero frequency = peripheral resistance (SVR, off scale). The mean modulus above ~2 Hz approximates characteristic impedance Zc (proximal aortic stiffness). The first harmonic Z1 tracks reflection amplitude, and the first minimum moves to higher frequency as reflections return earlier (≈ 1 / (2·Δtp)).
